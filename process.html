How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope. A simple AI chatbot can be live in 2-3 weeks. Comprehensive automation systems typically take 6-10 weeks. During our free strategy call, we'll provide a realistic timeline for your specific project.

Do I need technical knowledge?

Not at all. Grand Strand Digital handles all the technical work. We explain everything in plain language and train your team on any new tools. Our clients range from solo entrepreneurs to mid-size companies.

What's the investment range?

Projects start at $2,500 for focused solutions like chatbots or workflow automation. Comprehensive AI strategy and implementation ranges from $10,000-$50,000+. We always provide detailed proposals before any commitment.

Will AI replace my employees?

No — that's not our goal. AI handles repetitive, time-consuming tasks so your team can focus on high-value work. Think of it as giving your best people superpowers, not replacing them.

What happens after launch?

Grand Strand Digital provides ongoing support, monitoring, and optimization. AI systems improve over time with more data and feedback. We offer monthly retainer plans to keep your systems evolving with your business.

Do you work with businesses outside the Grand Strand?

Absolutely. While we're based in the Grand Strand and love working with local Carolinas businesses, we serve clients across the region and nationally. All of our work can be done remotely with the same quality and attention.
